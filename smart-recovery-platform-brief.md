# Hearthline: Smart Recovery and Prevention Platform

## Project Overview
Hearthline is a multi-modal, GenAI-powered recovery and prevention platform designed for individuals navigating substance use disorders, plus the caregivers who support them. The product focuses on zero-typing interventions, personalized emergency scripts, educational guidance, and contextual safety tools that reduce cognitive load during high-stress moments.

## Problem Statement
People in recovery and their support networks often need help when they are most overwhelmed, time-limited, or emotionally taxed. In those moments, typing long prompts, searching across resources, and deciding what to do next can be unrealistic. Hearthline addresses that gap by turning high-stress situations into guided, low-friction actions with fast access to crisis scripts, grounding steps, support resources, and caregiver-specific assistance.

## Core Product Goal
Deliver an end-to-end, real working application that helps users:
- identify a crisis or risk moment quickly,
- receive a personalized response path without typing,
- access an emergency script tailored to their situation,
- surface relevant educational resources,
- support caregivers with clear next-step guidance,
- reduce decision fatigue through structured, contextual UI.

## Primary User Roles
### 1. Individual in recovery
Needs immediate, low-friction support during cravings, relapse risk, emotional distress, or uncertainty.

### 2. Caregiver or family supporter
Needs guidance on how to respond, what to say, what not to do, and when to escalate.

### 3. Evaluator / reviewer
Needs to verify that every demoed feature works end-to-end with meaningful integrations and no fake flows.

## Proposed Application Features
### Zero-Typing Support Flow
A guided interface with large, tap-first options such as:
- I feel triggered
- I need help now
- Help me talk to someone
- I am supporting a loved one
- I want to prevent a future crisis

### Personalized Emergency Scripts
GenAI generates situational scripts for:
- calling a sponsor,
- telling a family member what is happening,
- asking for help without shame,
- de-escalating an argument,
- requesting immediate transport or supervision.

### Contextual Safety Tools
Depending on the selected situation, the app can show:
- grounding steps,
- delay tactics,
- safe contact shortcuts,
- crisis escalation options,
- check-in timers,
- recovery-friendly reminders.

### Educational Resource Hub
Short, readable guidance covering:
- what craving waves are,
- how to support someone without enabling,
- relapse warning signs,
- what to say during a difficult moment,
- how to build a personal safety plan.

### Caregiver Mode
A separate support path for caregivers with:
- suggested scripts,
- boundary-setting prompts,
- escalation guidance,
- reassurance content,
- a list of actions to avoid.

## Suggested GenAI Usage
To satisfy the requirement that GenAI is a real engine, use live model calls for:
- emergency script generation,
- personalized guidance based on the selected situation,
- resource summarization,
- tone adjustment for caregiver vs individual modes.
